What is CGPA?

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) represents your overall academic performance across all semesters completed so far. It is calculated by dividing total credit points earned across all semesters by the total credits attempted.

CGPA = (Sum of Credit Points of All Semesters) ÷ (Total Credits of All Semesters)

Example: If you score 720 total credit points with 80 total credits across 4 semesters, your CGPA = 720 ÷ 80 = 9.00

SGPA vs CGPA Comparison

SGPA
  • ✔ Scope: Single Semester
  • ✔ Purpose: Short-term performance
  • ✔ Updates: Every semester
  • ✔ Impact: Immediate feedback
CGPA
  • ✔ Scope: All Semesters
  • ✔ Purpose: Overall program performance
  • ✔ Updates: Cumulative record
  • ✔ Impact: Degree classification
